Apostle Imani Ministries is a growing Christian ministry committed to proclaiming Jesus Christ, making disciples, developing emerging Christian leaders, and equipping believers for the Great Commission.

About Apostle Imani

Apostle Imani is a Tanzanian Christian ministry leader, missionary, author, and prophet with a growing burden to see disciples of Jesus Christ become effective disciple-makers and emerging Christian leaders equipped for the Great Commission.

His ministry journey has been shaped by preaching, evangelism, discipleship, prayer and fasting, leadership encouragement, missionary engagement, and Christian literature.

The 2018 Mountain Experience

A significant foundation of Apostle Imani's ministry journey took place in 2018, when he withdrew to the mountains for 40 days and nights of fasting and prayer.

During that season, he experienced what he describes as a powerful baptism of the Holy Spirit that profoundly transformed his life. Returning from that season, he carried a deep and persistent fire for Jesus Christ, His Word, preaching the Gospel, and winning souls.

That encounter became one of the foundations of his ministry and continues to influence his understanding of calling, discipleship, leadership, prayer, and the Great Commission.

The journey from that encounter through the development of the ministry is documented in his book:

Divine Calling: The Journey of Apostle Imani and Friends of Faith

The book contains written accounts, testimonies, and photographic documentation connected with his calling, ministry experiences, and the people and circumstances through which he believes God has shaped his journey.

Ministry Vision

The vision of Apostle Imani Ministries extends beyond winning souls to making disciples who make disciples.

The ministry seeks to encourage a multiplying culture in which:

- Believers are grounded in God's Word.
- New disciples are equipped to disciple others.
- Emerging Christian leaders are developed.
- Local churches are strengthened for mission.
- Christians are encouraged to live out the Great Commission.
- The Gospel reaches communities and nations through multiplying disciples.

Missionary Experience

Apostle Imani's missionary journey has included service in the Philippines, providing cross-cultural ministry experience and strengthening his conviction that Christian leaders should be equipped not only to serve, but also to reproduce their faith and develop others.

This experience has contributed to a broader vision for discipleship, leadership development, missions, and the multiplication of Gospel workers.
